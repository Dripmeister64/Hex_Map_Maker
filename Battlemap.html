⬡ PLAYER VIEW

What you can do

  • Drag player and ally tokens to move them. The route and movement cost are shown.
  • Claim your mech: click it and press C (or Claim). Only you and the GM can then move it. Allies you deploy are yours straight away.
  • Right-drag anywhere to measure range and movement.
  • Line, Cone, Blast, Burst show what an attack would hit.
  • Deploy ally for drones, turrets and other deployables.
Hostile and neutral tokens are run by the GM. Click one to see what it is.

GM mode

Everything is unlocked. Hidden tokens look faded to you and are invisible to players. Lock the screen before handing it back.
